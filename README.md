# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis – Tableau Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **AdventureWorks Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, production costs, orders, products, customers, and regional performance.

The dashboard transforms business data into meaningful visual insights and provides an interactive view of key business metrics through filters, KPIs, and multiple visualizations.

This project demonstrates practical experience in **Tableau, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, KPI Development, and Business Intelligence**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track total sales, profit, production cost, and orders.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Compare sales with production costs.
- Analyze quarterly and yearly sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Identify top customers by sales.
- Compare sales across different territories.
- Calculate and monitor profit margin.
- Create an interactive and easy-to-understand Tableau dashboard.
- Generate meaningful business insights from the data.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales vs Production Cost Over Time

A combined visualization is used to compare sales and production costs over time.

This helps analyze:

- Monthly sales trends
- Production cost trends
- Relationship between sales and production costs
- Changes in business performance over time

---

### 2. Quarter-Wise Sales

A quarter-wise visualization shows the contribution of each quarter to total sales.

The dashboard provides a comparison between:

- Q1
- Q2
- Q3
- Q4

This helps identify the contribution of different quarters to overall business sales.

---

### 3. Year-Wise Sales

The dashboard compares sales across different years.

This allows users to:

- Identify high-performing years
- Compare year-wise revenue
- Understand long-term sales trends

---

### 4. Month-Wise Sales

Monthly sales are displayed using a trend chart.

The analysis helps identify:

- Monthly sales growth
- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Seasonal patterns

---

### 5. Top 3 Products by Sales

The dashboard identifies the top three products based on sales.

This helps understand which products contribute significantly to revenue.

---

### 6. Top 3 Customers by Sales

The dashboard displays the top three customers based on sales.

This provides insight into major customers and their contribution to the business.

---

### 7. Top 3 Sales Territory Regions

The dashboard compares sales across different territories.

The displayed dashboard highlights:

- Australia
- Southwest
- Northwest

This helps understand geographical sales performance.
